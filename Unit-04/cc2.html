Welcome to Sara's Kitchen

Sara lives in Brooklyn, NY, but her heart is in Trinidad and Tobago. Every summer, she visits her grandparents and learns traditional family recipes.

Sara's Favorite Foods

  • Doubles (fried dough with curry chickpeas)
  • Bake and Shark (fried bread with seasoned shark)
  • Pelau (rice and pigeon peas with meat)
  • Callaloo (creamy spinach-like soup)
  • Aloo Pie (fried pastry filled with spiced potatoes)

How to Make Doubles

  1. Prepare the dough and let it rise for 2 hours
  2. Make the curry chana (chickpeas) with traditional spices
  3. Roll the dough into small balls and flatten them
  4. Deep fry the flattened dough until golden brown
  5. Top with curry chana, tamarind sauce, and pepper
  6. Serve warm and enjoy!

Traditional Ingredients Explained

Curry Chana
Spiced chickpeas cooked in a rich curry sauce, the main filling for doubles.
Tamarind Sauce
A sweet and tangy sauce made from tamarind fruit, often drizzled on top of doubles.
Bara
The fried flatbread used to hold the curry chana in doubles.
